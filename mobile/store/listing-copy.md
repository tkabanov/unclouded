## Full description

Unclouded is an AI-powered coaching companion built for people navigating
burnout, career pressure, grief, and personal growth. A personalized
assessment maps your current state across stability, performance, and
alignment, then pairs you with Kota — a coaching AI trained to meet you
where you are.

What's inside:
- A personalized assessment and classification, with a 90-day reassessment
  to track your progress over time.
- Unlimited AI coaching conversations (Free tier includes 7 sessions/month).
- Guided coaching paths across emotional, professional, and health &
  wellness pillars.
- Daily check-ins to build a habit of noticing how you're doing.
- Deep-dive modules ("Know Yourself Deeper") that build a fuller picture of
  your identity, relationships, and history.
- A journal with optional AI reflection.
- Premium: 1:1 human coaching sessions and group coaching, on top of
  everything else.

Unclouded is a coaching tool, not a substitute for therapy or medical care.
If you're in crisis, the app will always point you to the 988 Suicide &
Crisis Lifeline and the Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741).

Manage your subscription any time at unclouded.app.
